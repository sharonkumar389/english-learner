Voices

Active Voice and Passive Voice

Active Voice

In Active Voice, the subject performs the action.

Example:
Ravi writes a letter.

Passive Voice

In Passive Voice, the subject receives the action.

Example:
A letter is written by Ravi.

Speeches

Direct Speech and Indirect Speech

Direct Speech

Direct Speech reports the exact words spoken by someone.

Example:
Ravi said, "I am tired."

Indirect Speech

Indirect Speech reports what someone said without using the exact words.

Example:
Ravi said that he was tired.

Parts of Speech

The eight major parts of speech

Noun

A word that names a person, place, thing or idea.

Pronoun

A word used instead of a noun.

Verb

A word that expresses an action or state.

Adjective

A word that describes a noun or pronoun.

Adverb

A word that modifies a verb, adjective or adverb.

Preposition

A word that shows a relationship between words.

Conjunction

A word that connects words or sentences.

Interjection

A word expressing sudden emotion.

12 Tenses

Learn the structures of English tenses.

Tense Structure Example
Simple Present Subject + V1 I play cricket.
Present Continuous Subject + am/is/are + V-ing I am playing cricket.
Present Perfect Subject + has/have + V3 I have played cricket.
Present Perfect Continuous Subject + has/have been + V-ing I have been playing cricket.
Simple Past Subject + V2 I played cricket.
Past Continuous Subject + was/were + V-ing I was playing cricket.
Past Perfect Subject + had + V3 I had played cricket.
Past Perfect Continuous Subject + had been + V-ing I had been playing cricket.
Simple Future Subject + will + V1 I will play cricket.
Future Continuous Subject + will be + V-ing I will be playing cricket.
Future Perfect Subject + will have + V3 I will have played cricket.
Future Perfect Continuous Subject + will have been + V-ing I will have been playing cricket.
